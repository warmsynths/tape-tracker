Pictures of the Tracker's stock screens shown by the dimmed buttons in the emulator.
Name them params.png, config.png, sample-load.png, sample-play.png, sample-rec.png (800 x 480; BMP or PNG).
On the Tracker: open the screen, hold Shift and press the Power button; the picture is saved on the SD card as Snapshots/Snapshot_NNN.bmp.
Without a picture the emulator shows a placeholder card.
