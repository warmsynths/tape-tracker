Eight sounds are built in. Load your own onto a pad slot:

R, Space, SRec, Play, Stop
Arrowsarrow buttons
[ ] or scrolljog wheel
Enterpress the jog wheel
Shift (hold)Shift
E, G, M, FSample Edit, Performance, Master, File

Try this

  1. Press Rec (R), let it play a few seconds, press Stop (S).
  2. Choose the bars with the jog wheel, press button 8 (OK).
  3. Press button 6 for track 2, then Rec: it waits for the loop start and records one loop.
  4. Shift + button 4 sets where each track records from. Shift + button 2 opens the pads.
  5. G opens Glitch (hold buttons 1 to 4), M the lo-fi master, F projects and export.
