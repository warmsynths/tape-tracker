On the device

What to press on the Tracker. B1 to B8 are the eight buttons under the screen.

Recording
B1 or RecRecord. A count-in, then the first take. Stop ends it and asks how many bars.
B2 TempoBefore the first take: starting tempo. Once there is a loop, B2 is Stop.
B3 or PlayPlay the loop
B4Monitor the input on or off
B5 to B8Select Track 1 to 4. Press the selected one again to open it large.
Shift + UpUndo the last take
Setting the BPM (B2 Tempo)
B1 FreeNo set tempo: the first take sets the loop and the tempo is worked out from its length
B2 SetTakes land on a grid at the tempo you choose
JogChange the BPM by 0.1 (Shift: by 1), 40 to 240
Left, RightChoose Tempo, Count-in or Wait for sound; Jog changes it, Insert switches it
B7 OK, B8B7 keeps it, B8 cancels
Shift + PlayClick (metronome) on or off. A CLICK tag shows in the top bar.
Shift on the home screen
Shift + B1Loop mode: Repeat or Play once
Shift + B2Pads (what the pads do)
Shift + B3Memory (recording time left)
Shift + B4Input: choose a track's source (Line, mics, Pads)
Shift + B5 to B8Mute Track 1 to 4
Shift + DownSettings (project: click, grid, snap, count-in)
Shift + Left, RightOutput volume
Shift + JogLevel of the selected track (1 dB a click)
Left, RightLevel of the synth (chord pads), 1 dB a click
InsertActions for the selected Part (move, edit, glitch, mute, undo)
Shift + InsertMove the Part (Jog steps by the grid, Shift by 1 ms)
Buttons beside the screen
ParamsTrack mixer: level, pan, tone, mute, solo
PerformGlitch effects for the selected track
Sample EditPart Editor: trim, fades, offset, grid
MasterLo-fi master (tape saturation, glue, wow). It starts off: switch it on here.
FileProjects: save, load, export WAV
ConfigDevice settings, firmware update
Sample LoadLoad WAV files from the SD card onto the pads
Sample PlaySettings of the pad sample (tune, volume, start, end)
Sample RecResample the mix into a pad slot
CopyCopy the selected Part to another track. Shift + Copy undoes a copy or clear.
DeleteClear the selected Part. Shift + Delete: all tracks, or Reset all.
PatternBack to the home screen from anywhere
Chords and sound
NoteChords page. The pads play chords and scale notes. With them on, Rec records them (the track's source becomes Pads).
Chords pageLeft, Right: choose a setting; Jog changes it. B1 pads on or off, B2 Shuffle, B3 Reset, B4 Sound, B7 next page (harmony, chord types, feel), B8 back.
Right two pad columnsOct +, Oct -, Invert, Open, Sus, 7th: hold while you play a chord. Hold keeps the chord going. The last pad steps the play style (Block, Strum, Arp).
InstrSound page: pick a patch, tweak the eight knobs, hold B1 to hear it, B7 for every setting
